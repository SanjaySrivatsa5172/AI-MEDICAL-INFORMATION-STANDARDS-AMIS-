Physician-Authored Evaluation of Patient-Facing AI in Emergency Triage

Why today's AI-triage evaluations fail — and how this study fixes each

01 · Format
Multiple-choice benchmarks reward memorization; patients type free text.
Fraile Navarro 2026 · Singh (FreeMedQA): −39 pts MCQ→free-response
Fix: free-text scenarios in paired lay + expert register.
02 · Clinical construct
Triage is a 2–5 min, present-information-only decision — not a specialist workup.
Ramaswamy 2026 · Nat Med: 51.6% of emergencies under-triaged
Fix: constrained triage-moment prompts; undertriage as primary endpoint.
03 · Contamination
Test items leak into training data; models recycle their own output.
"Emperor's New Clothes" 2025 · LiveMedBench (contamination-free)
Fix: offline, local model weights, no internet retrieval at test time.
04 · Scoring
Accuracy ≠ safety, and under-triage is far more dangerous than over-triage.

Fix: harm-weighted, asymmetric, physician-adjudicated scoring.

Briefing note · S. Sanjay Srivatsa, MD (PI)

Incremental value: what an independent triage-safety study measures that a deployed diagnostic-support system cannot

A live diagnostic-support deployment and an independent triage-safety evaluation answer different questions. The deployment improves care with clean, physician-mediated input. This study measures what happens when input is lay, incomplete, and unfiltered — the condition under which the public actually uses AI, and the one condition no deployed system can assess about itself.

1What this study uniquely answers

  • Triage safety under lay-register input — not physician-distilled EHR data, but "worst headache of my life" with the red flag buried in prose.
  • Input variance / register-dependence — does identical clinical content, worded lay vs. expert, change the triage? A consistency property a deployment never runs in.
  • Critical-miss rate at the triage snapshot — present-information-only, where under-triage delays or precludes life-saving care.

2Why an active deployment is the reason to do this — not to skip it

Deployment ≠ validation. A system diagnosing a hard case correctly (e.g. eosinophilic esophagitis with structured input) demonstrates capability, not triage safety under real-world input.
  • Safety is a distinct axis from accuracy.NOHARM (Stanford/Harvard): clinical safety correlated only r≈0.6 with accuracy/knowledge benchmarks — strong benchmarks do not predict safe recommendations. arXiv:2512.01241
  • Physician-mediated input is a categorically easier condition.Reis et al., Nature Health: patients give chatbots measurably lower-quality symptom reports than they give physicians. A deployment reading distilled EHR data never faces the input the public generates. 10.1038/s44360-026-00116-y
  • Independent stress-testing still finds danger as products ship.Ramaswamy et al., Nat Med: 51.6% of gold-standard emergencies under-triaged; the fatal-PE Winters v. OpenAI suit shows the downstream stakes. 10.1038/s41591-026-04297-7

3Why the method survives methodological review

  • Dual-register scenarios (paired lay + expert) isolate input variance — the confound Fraile Navarro (2026) shows can move headline results.
  • Offline / local weights / no live retrieval removes the contamination and internet-lookup confounds that undermine most prior benchmarks.
  • Harm-weighted, under-triage-asymmetric scoring, physician-adjudicated and benchmarked against an independent (non-author) physician panel and an AI panel — mirroring NOHARM's validated panel design (autograder vs. inter-physician κ ≈ 0.80).
  • Three arms — model-alone · physician-alone · human-AI hybrid — and Cedars' own deployed system can serve as the real-world hybrid arm, making the study incremental and directly useful to Cedars.

4The ask

  • Frame: AI-safety evaluation science & governance — a reusable, publishable harness Cedars can use to validate and govern the triage AI it already runs (not an audit of it).
  • People: a PhD student (spring project window) + a methodologist / biostatistician co-lead + an ED physician champion to author scenarios and staff the independent benchmark.
  • Why now: regulatory attention, live litigation, and a converging literature (Ramaswamy, NOHARM, Fraile Navarro, Reis, Weilnhammer) make an independent triage-safety instrument timely and fundable.
