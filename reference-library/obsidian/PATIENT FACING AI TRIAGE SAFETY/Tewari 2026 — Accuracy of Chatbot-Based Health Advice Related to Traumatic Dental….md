# Accuracy of Chatbot-Based Health Advice Related to Traumatic Dental Injuries: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**Tewari N, Kidwai AA, Wadhwani E, et al.** — *Dental Traumatology* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Systematic review + meta-analysis (Cochrane Handbook + CHART reporting tool, OSF-registered) of contemporary consumer chatbots answering patient questions about emergency care of traumatic dental injuries -- a time-critical presentation (e.g., avulsion has a short replantation window). 10 studies (2024-2025), 7 chatbots; pooled accuracy 65% (95% CI 52-78) for ChatGPT-3.5, 79% (61-93) for ChatGPT-4.0, 70% (60-79) for Gemini, with high heterogeneity (I2 ~86-89%). Authors conclude accuracy is only moderate and highly question-dependent and explicitly caution these figures 'should not be interpreted as evidence of clinical reliability, as even limited inaccuracies in emergency dental trauma advice may contribute to delayed or inappropriate management and adverse outcomes.' Topic 2 / patient-facing-AI: consumer-chatbot advice accuracy and its safety ceiling for an urgent, everyday-language health question.
