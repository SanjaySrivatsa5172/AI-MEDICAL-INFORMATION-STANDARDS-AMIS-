# Reducing Under-Triage Risk in Large Language Model Based Clinical Triage Using UMLS-CUI Augmentation

**Gokhale R, Kukreja M, Kumar N, Gourab K** — *medRxiv preprint* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Reuses the same 60 clinician-authored vignette x 16 factorial-condition harness (960 combinations) as the standing Ramaswamy/Fraile Navarro benchmark to test whether augmenting LLM prompts with UMLS Concept Unique Identifiers reduces undertriage; emergency-case accuracy improved from 73.0% to 80.7% (GPT-4o-mini) and 60.5% to 68.5% (MedGemma 27B), with CUI augmentation reducing susceptibility to anchoring statements. First mitigation-focused study built on the same evaluation architecture as this protocol and the standing undertriage alerts -- flagged as urgent alert.
