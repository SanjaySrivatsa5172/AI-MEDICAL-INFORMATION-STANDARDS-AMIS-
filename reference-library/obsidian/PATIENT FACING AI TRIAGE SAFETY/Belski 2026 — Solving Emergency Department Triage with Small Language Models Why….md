# Solving Emergency Department Triage with Small Language Models: Why Large Commercial Models Fail and How Specialized Training Achieves Clinical Accuracy

**Belski V, Lukina K** — *medRxiv preprint* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Compares large commercial LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro, MedGemma) against a purpose-built small-model pipeline (BiomedBERT extraction + deterministic clinical engine, plus a 9B-parameter chain-of-thought/RL-trained model) for Emergency Severity Index (ESI) assignment; off-the-shelf large models reach only 45-55% exact ESI accuracy, versus 88.9% exact / 97.2% adjacent (+/-1 ESI) accuracy for the specialized pipeline on a 50-case expert-labeled set. Supports the supporting-strand benchmark-methodology/ESI-ground-truth angle; not peer-reviewed and lower priority (general triage-model engineering rather than physician-authored patient-facing evaluation).
