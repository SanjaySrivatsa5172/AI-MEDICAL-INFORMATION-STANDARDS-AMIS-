> [!note] Clinical relevance
> CLOSEST design parallel to this protocol surfaced to date: a physician-panel-adjudicated, PATIENT-FACING symptom-guidance triage-safety validation with prespecified undertriage/overtriage endpoints. Predeployment analytical validation of a locked configuration of Verily Health's Personal Health Assistant (PHA), an LLM-enabled patient-facing symptom-guidance tool: 772 synthetic cases built from published TELEPHONE-TRIAGE protocols plus persona-based, LLM-assisted case generation, each with a clinical vignette, structured history, and a simulated MULTITURN patient conversation; a panel of 5 independent physicians (BLINDED to PHA outputs) set case-level level-of-care ground truth via prespecified plurality/tie-handling rules. Against prespecified criteria: urgent undertriage 6.9% (95% CI 4.6-9.8; criterion <5% -- NOT met), nonurgent undertriage 18.4% (95% CI 14.2-23.2; criterion <15% -- NOT met), overtriage 11.0% (95% CI 8.0-14.7; criterion <30% -- met); undertriage was identified as the principal performance gap. This shares the protocol's physician-panel-adjudicated, patient-facing, undertriage/overtriage-endpoint design and telephone-triage-protocol case basis -- the study should CITE AND DIFFERENTIATE on the axes that keep it distinct: physician-AUTHORED NEW scenarios (vs synthetic/persona-generated), pre-specified DUAL-REGISTER lay-vs-clinician presentation, and HARM-WEIGHTED / Critical-Miss-Rate primary scoring.
