# ChatGPT Health performance in a structured test of triage recommendations

**Ramaswamy A, Tyagi A, Hugo H, et al.** — *Nature Medicine* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> First independent safety evaluation of ChatGPT Health: 60 clinician-authored vignettes across 21 domains under 16 factorial conditions (960 responses); inverted U-shaped failure pattern, 52% undertriage of gold-standard emergencies (DKA, impending respiratory failure), inconsistent suicide-crisis safeguard activation, and significant anchoring-bias shifts when family/friends minimized symptoms
