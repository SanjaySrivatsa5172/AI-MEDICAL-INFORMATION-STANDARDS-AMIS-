# MIRA: A Bilingual Benchmark for Medical Information Response Audit

**Xu M, Yang Q, Wang Q, Dai X, Wu W, Gao C** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Bilingual controlled benchmark (4,320 prompts built from 60 medically-reviewed low-risk health questions) testing whether LLMs give comparable medical information across user-side language/register/health-literacy signals; identifies 'Differential Information Dilution' -- across 5 mainstream LLMs, responses to low-health-literacy phrasing consistently omitted more key information and offered fewer concrete next steps. Direct evidence on the Core Question Anchor's health-literacy dimension.
