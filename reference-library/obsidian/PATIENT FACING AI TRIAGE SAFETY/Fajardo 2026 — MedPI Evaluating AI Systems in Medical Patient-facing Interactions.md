# MedPI: Evaluating AI Systems in Medical Patient-facing Interactions

**Fajardo V D, Proniakin O, Gruber VE, Marinescu R** — *medRxiv preprint* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> High-dimensional benchmark for LLM patient-clinician conversations: synthetic EHR-grounded 'AI Patients' with memory/affect are interviewed by 9 flagship models (Claude Opus 4.1, Claude Sonnet 4, MedGemma, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-5, GPT-OSS-120B, o3, Grok-4) across 366 AI patients / 7,097 conversations, scored on a 105-dimension ACGME-competency-aligned rubric by calibrated committee-based AI Judges; low performance concentrated in differential-diagnosis dimensions. Directly on Topic 2's evaluation-framework/accreditation-aligned-rubric architecture for patient-facing AI, though the 'physicians' in the grading loop are AI Judges rather than human physicians -- a methodological point of contrast with this protocol's physician-authored design.
