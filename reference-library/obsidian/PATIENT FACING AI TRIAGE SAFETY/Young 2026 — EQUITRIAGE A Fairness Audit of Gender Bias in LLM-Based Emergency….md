# EQUITRIAGE: A Fairness Audit of Gender Bias in LLM-Based Emergency Department Triage

**Young RJ, Matthews AM** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Fairness audit of five LLMs (Gemini-3-Flash, Nemotron-3-Super, DeepSeek-V3.1, Mistral-Small-3.2, GPT-4.1-Nano) assigning ESI acuity to 18,714 MIMIC-IV-ED-derived vignettes (374,275 evaluations) under baseline/chain-of-thought/debiased/blind prompting -- all five exceeded a pre-registered 5% acuity-flip threshold (9.9%-43.8%), two showed directional female undertriage (DeepSeek F/M 2.15:1, Gemini 1.34:1), and demographic blinding sharply reduced flip rates, implicating identity cues (with age as a residual channel) as a driver of triage instability independent of clinical presentation -- directly relevant to this protocol's undertriage/mistriage safety endpoint.
