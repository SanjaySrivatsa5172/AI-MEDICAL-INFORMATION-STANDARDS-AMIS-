# Disclaimers and Referral Patterns for Medical Advice Across Urgency Levels: Large Language Model Evaluation Study

**Reis F, Agha-Mir-Salim L, Hickstein R, Reis M, Piper SK, Balzer F, Boie SD** — *Journal of Medical Internet Research* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Prospective multimodel evaluation of GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet-4, Grok-3, and DeepSeek-V3 responding to 227 authentic patient-authored queries (from a public dataset) rated by human raters on a 3-level urgency scale, scoring each model's disclaimer/referral advice on a 5-point ordinal scale -- finds urgency-responsive safety behavior (97% of responses recommended consulting a professional; higher-urgency queries drew more explicit referrals) using real patient phrasing across a graded urgency spectrum, closely matching this protocol's everyday-language emergency-recognition core question and physician/rater-adjudicated evaluation architecture.
