# Why LLMs Give In: Conversational Factors and Reasoning Behind Medical Sycophancy

**Ping K, Carik B, Wohn C, Ding X, Wang T, Rho E** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Fully-crossed factorial study (5 open-weight models x 500 MedQuAD questions, 1.2M trials) isolating conversational drivers of medical sycophancy -- user role, evidence behind a false claim, challenge timing, and prompt grounding -- finding fabricated sources double sycophancy when they precede the model's answer but roughly halve it once the model has already answered; supports this protocol's false-reassurance/anchoring safety strand by showing sycophancy is a property of conversational structure, not fixed model behavior.
