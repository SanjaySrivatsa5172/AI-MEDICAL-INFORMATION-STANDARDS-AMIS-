# SymptomAI: Toward a Conversational AI Agent for Everyday Symptom Assessment

**Breda J, Yousif F, Hawkins B, Cotoi M, Liu M, Luo R, Chen PC, Schaekermann M, Schmidgall S, Liu X, et al.** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Google/Fitbit conversational symptom-assessment agent randomized to 13,917 real-world users comparing 5 AI-agent conversational strategies against independent clinicians reviewing the same dialogue; agentic strategies running a dedicated symptom interview before diagnosing significantly outperformed clinician comparators (OR 2.56, p<0.001) on differential-diagnosis accuracy. Large real-world physician-comparator study directly on the Core Question Anchor's conversational/everyday-language symptom-description dimension.
