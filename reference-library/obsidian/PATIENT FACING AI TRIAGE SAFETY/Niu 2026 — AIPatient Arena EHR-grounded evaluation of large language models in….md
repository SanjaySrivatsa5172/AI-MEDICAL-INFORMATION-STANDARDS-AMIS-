# AIPatient Arena: EHR-grounded evaluation of large language models in end-to-end clinical consultation workflows

**Niu J, Yu H, Wang W, Dai G, He J, Li X, Liang Z, Lin X, So KCY, Yan BYP, Wing YK, Xing Y, Ma X, Fan L** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Shandong University / Chinese University of Hong Kong EHR-grounded evaluation framework integrating patient-specific knowledge graphs into multi-turn LLM consultations, scored across 8 clinical-competence dimensions on a primary cohort of 437 patients plus two out-of-distribution validation cohorts (n=119, n=67); models scored well on interview questioning and professionalism (4.4-5.0/5) but weak on handling ambiguous patient responses (2.6-3.3/5), information coverage (2.1-3.0/5), and diagnostic accuracy/reasoning (2.6-3.6/5). Supporting-strand hit on Topic 2's rubric-based patient-facing AI evaluation methodology; general clinical consultation rather than ED-triage-specific.
