> [!note] Clinical relevance

Klang senior -- the same Mount Sinai/Klang group as the library's CARE-Bench entry) arguing LLM medical-consultation benchmarks are evaluated TOO LATE: they begin after the clinical problem is already clearly framed, whereas real consultations start from a vague, minimized, or misframed concern (the 'PREFORMULATION GAP').
