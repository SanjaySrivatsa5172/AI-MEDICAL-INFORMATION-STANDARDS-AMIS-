# Characteristics and Safety of Consumer Chatbots for Emergent Adolescent Health Concerns

**Brewster RCL, Zahedivash A, Tse G, Bourgeois F, Hadland SE** — *JAMA Network Open* (2025)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Cross-sectional audit of consumer chatbots (general-assistant and companion types) examining content policies and simulated behavior in response to adolescent health crises, including emergent/self-harm scenarios. General-assistant chatbots more consistently included self-harm content policies than companion chatbots (100% vs 46.7%). Bears on Topic 1 (emergency triage AI safety) and the consumer-product-name search angle for a vulnerable patient-facing population; predates this protocol's 2026-08-02 seed sweep but was not previously captured.
