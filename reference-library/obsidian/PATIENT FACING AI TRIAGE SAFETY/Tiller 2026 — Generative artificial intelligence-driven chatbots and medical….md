# Generative artificial intelligence-driven chatbots and medical misinformation: an accuracy, referencing and readability audit

**Tiller NB, Marcon AR, Zenone M, et al.** — *BMJ Open* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Five popular consumer chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek) audited on 250 health-information questions across cancer, vaccines, stem cells, nutrition, and athletic performance; 49.6% of responses were somewhat/highly problematic and no chatbot produced a fully accurate reference list -- consumer-product-name angle supporting evidence on patient-facing AI reliability (not triage-specific).
