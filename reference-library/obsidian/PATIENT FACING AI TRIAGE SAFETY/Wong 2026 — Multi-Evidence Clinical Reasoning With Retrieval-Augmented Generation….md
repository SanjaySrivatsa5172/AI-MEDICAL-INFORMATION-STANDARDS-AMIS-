# Multi-Evidence Clinical Reasoning With Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Emergency Triage: Retrospective Evaluation Study

**Wong HS, Wong TK** — *JMIR Medical Informatics* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Hong Kong ED physicians' dual-source RAG system (grounded in the Hong Kong Accident & Emergency Triage Guidelines plus 3,000 retrieved past ED encounters) evaluated retrospectively against triage-nurse ground truth: mean QWK 0.902 / accuracy 0.802 vs. prompt-only baseline LLM QWK 0.801 / accuracy 0.542, reducing 3-group overtriage from 28.8% (baseline LLM) to 12.7% while holding undertriage at 1.3%. Clinician decision-support tool rather than patient-facing lay-language AI, but directly on Topic 1's undertriage/overtriage measurement architecture and a useful guideline-grounding comparator for the protocol's own triage-accuracy metrics.
