# Few-Shot Large Language Models for Actionable Triage Categorization of Online Patient Inquiries

**Zhou L, Li J** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Using the public HealthCareMagic-100K corpus of informal patient-authored inquiries, benchmarks few-shot/zero-shot LLMs and supervised TF-IDF/BioBERT baselines on a four-class actionable triage task (self-care / schedule-visit / urgent-clinician-review / emergency-referral), evaluated with safety-aware triage metrics and model-disagreement as a human-in-the-loop selective-review signal -- a low-resource, real-world-phrasing triage categorization setting adjacent to this protocol's everyday-language emergency-recognition question.
