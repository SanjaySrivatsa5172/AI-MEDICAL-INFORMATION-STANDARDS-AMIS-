# This Treatment Works, Right? Evaluating LLM Sensitivity to Patient Question Framing in Medical QA

**Yun HS, Kapoor G, Mackert M, Kouzy R, Xu W, Li JJ, Wallace BC** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Controlled RAG medical-QA study: 6,614 query pairs varying patient question framing (positive vs. negative) and language style (technical vs. plain) grounded in clinical-trial abstracts, evaluated across 8 LLMs; positively- and negatively-framed pairs produced significantly more contradictory conclusions than same-framing pairs. Direct evidence on the Core Question Anchor's lay-language/register-effect dimension.
