# Generative AI for Establishing the Chief Complaint

**Sorin V, Klang E** — *Journal of Clinical Medicine* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Physician-authored VIEWPOINT (Vera Sorin & Eyal Klang -- Mayo Clinic / Beth Israel Deaconess, Harvard) proposing an evaluation framework for patient-facing GENERATIVE-AI symptom INTAKE and chief-complaint establishment. Squarely on the protocol's core question: patients 'may struggle to describe their symptoms or leave important concerns unspoken,' and the authors propose measuring 'the proportion of patients whose main symptoms or concerns are missing or incorrectly described after AI-assisted intake' -- explicitly including whether 'concerns requiring URGENT assessment were missed,' so the AI-produced summary helps clinicians 'judge urgency and decide what needs attention.' Relevant via emergency-relevance (triage / judging urgency / missed urgent concerns) + patient-facing (AI conversational symptom intake with lay patients, in their own words) + AI-safety FAILURE-MODE (missed-urgent-concern / under-capture of red flags).
