# The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Enhancing Diagnostic and Triage Accuracy in Hospital Emergency Departments: A Systematic Review

**Shakoori M, Esmaili F, Abbariki E, Heidaranlu E** — *Medical Journal of the Islamic Republic of Iran* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> SYSTEMATIC REVIEW (Iran University of Medical Sciences / Baqiyatallah University of Medical Sciences; PubMed/Scopus/WoS/Google Scholar to Sept 2025; NOS + QUADAS-2) of AI for TRIAGE and diagnostic accuracy in hospital EMERGENCY DEPARTMENTS. AI models often outperformed traditional triage (AUC frequently >0.90), but the review flags AUC's limits in imbalanced data and -- directly on this protocol's failure-mode focus -- notes that ChatGPT 4.0 produced significant OVER-TRIAGE from low specificity (18.2%), alongside algorithmic bias, workflow-integration friction, user resistance, and the need for continuous ethical oversight. Concludes AI could improve emergency triage but current evidence is largely retrospective single-center, and implementation should be gradual, starting in high-risk domains (e.g., sepsis screening). Relevant as an ED-triage-focused evidence synthesis that explicitly surfaces a consumer-LLM (ChatGPT) triage safety failure (over-triage) plus algorithmic-bias / ethical-oversight themes.
