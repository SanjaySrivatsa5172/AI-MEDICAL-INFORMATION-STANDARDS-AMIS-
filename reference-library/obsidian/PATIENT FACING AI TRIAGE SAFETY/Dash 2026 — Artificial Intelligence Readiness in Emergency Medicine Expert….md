# Artificial Intelligence Readiness in Emergency Medicine: Expert Consensus Opinion for Preparing the Workforce

**Dash D, Macalalad J, Lum DL, Baker MW, Dalsey WC, Sangal RB, Dayton JR, Waseem TC, Sikka NK, Chu AL.** — *Journal of the American College of Emergency Physicians Open* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Authoritative PHYSICIAN-AUTHORED governance/evaluation consensus for emergency-medicine AI -- the official work product of the American College of Emergency Physicians (ACEP) AI Task Force, formally endorsed by the ACEP Board of Directors in 2025. Directly relevant to this protocol's central premise that physician-authored evaluation of AI used in the emergency-care pathway is needed and currently under-resourced: the consensus states that AI integration into ED workflows -- spanning TRIAGE, imaging, clinical decision support, documentation, and operations -- has OUTPACED clinician training, vendor-evaluation infrastructure, and specialty-wide governance, leaving most emergency physicians without the foundational skills to critically evaluate or safely oversee these products, and no national standard to distinguish safe tools from risky ones. It proposes three coordinated priorities: EDUCATE (a standardized AI-education framework from residency through CME, anchored in specialty competencies); EVALUATE (an expert-derived, structured, CLINICIAN-LED THREE-STAGE FRAMEWORK for vetting AI tools and industry partners before deployment); and ADVISE (a national Emergency Medicine AI Advisory Council for shared terminology, best-practice guidance, and implementation toolkits). Bears on the protocol as the specialty-endorsed statement of the evaluation/governance gap that a physician-authored, harm-weighted evaluation of patient-facing emergency-triage AI helps fill -- a citable anchor for the background/rationale (why clinician-led pre-deployment evaluation of triage AI matters) and for situating the study's contribution within ACEP's Evaluate pillar. topics emergency-triage-ai / policy-regulation / physician-evaluation.
