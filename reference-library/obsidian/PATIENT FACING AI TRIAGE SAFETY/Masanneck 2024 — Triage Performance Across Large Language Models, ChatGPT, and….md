# Triage Performance Across Large Language Models, ChatGPT, and Untrained Doctors in Emergency Medicine: Comparative Study

**Masanneck L, Schmidt L, Seifert A, et al.** — *Journal of Medical Internet Research* (2024)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Comparative study (Manchester Triage System): 124 case vignettes triaged by untrained doctors, several LLMs (raw GPT-4, Llama 3 70B, Gemini 1.5, Mixtral 8x7b) and ChatGPT (GPT-3.5/GPT-4) against a professional-rater consensus gold standard, scored by quadratic-weighted Cohen kappa. GPT-4-based ChatGPT (kappa 0.67) matched untrained ED doctors (0.68) and beat GPT-3.5 (0.54), but none reached professional-rater level; LLMs/ChatGPT tended to OVER-triage whereas untrained doctors UNDER-triaged, and LLM second-opinion did not significantly improve untrained doctors' triage. Direct LLM-vs-human emergency-triage comparator bearing on Topics 1 & 3 (over/undertriage endpoints, physician-consensus grading). Surfaced via the digital.health/journals coverage expansion (JMIR); previously uncaptured.
