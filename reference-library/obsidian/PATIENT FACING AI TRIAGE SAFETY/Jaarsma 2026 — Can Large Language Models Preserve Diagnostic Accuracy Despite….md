# Can Large Language Models Preserve Diagnostic Accuracy Despite Patient Self-Diagnosis and Framing Bias in Hypothetical Upper-Extremity Scenarios?

**Jaarsma EH, Ring D, Wickman J, Drost A.** — *The Journal of Hand Surgery* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> A named-failure-mode (ANCHORING / patient self-diagnosis / framing-bias) study that provides a CONTRASTING NULL to the standing Sheppert anchoring finding (added 2026-08-17), which reported LLMs anchor on a lay-stated cue far more than physicians. Here ChatGPT (GPT-5) evaluated 180 randomized hypothetical vignettes across five common upper-extremity conditions (de Quervain tendinopathy, rotator cuff tendinopathy, lateral epicondylitis, trigger digit, trapeziometacarpal arthritis), each vignette carrying randomized patient characteristics, characteristic-vs-VAGUE symptom descriptions, and a PATIENT SELF-DIAGNOSIS (correct / plausible alternative / common misconception). Result: the model identified the intended diagnosis in 165/180 (92%); accuracy was UNAFFECTED by the patient's self-diagnosis (higher for characteristic than vague symptoms; lower for de Quervain); it deviated from the patient's proposed diagnosis in 117/180 (65%), of which 104 (89%) appropriately aligned with the intended diagnosis, and it was more likely to disregard patient-provided diagnoses that did not match the intended one. Authors conclude GPT-5 showed LIMITED susceptibility to the anchoring/confirmation/acquiescence biases known to affect human reasoning and could support debiasing / correcting patient misconceptions. Bears on the protocol's anchoring/false-reassurance and lay-language self-diagnosis dimensions, and is valuable for balance: the library now holds both an anchoring-VULNERABLE result (Sheppert, forced-choice IM DDx, LLMs anchor > physicians) and this anchoring-RESISTANT result (single newest-model, open-ended DDx). CAVEAT: condition-specific upper-extremity MUSCULOSKELETAL diagnostic accuracy (NOT emergency/urgency triage or a harm-weighted endpoint), hypothetical vignettes, single model (GPT-5), and a FAVORABLE direction -- included as an anchoring/self-diagnosis named-failure-mode reference (per the Sheppert precedent), not as a triage-safety or dual-register study. design_overlap FALSE; NOT alerted (favorable/null, condition-specific).
