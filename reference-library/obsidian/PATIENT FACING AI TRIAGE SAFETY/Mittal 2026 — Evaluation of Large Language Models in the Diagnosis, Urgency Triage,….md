# Evaluation of Large Language Models in the Diagnosis, Urgency Triage, and Initial Management of Ophthalmic Emergencies

**Mittal S, Aggarwal Y** — *Cureus* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> 40 standardized ophthalmic-emergency vignettes across five subspecialties tested against ChatGPT-5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and Claude Opus 4.1; all three exceeded 80% diagnostic accuracy with guideline-concordant management advice and no significant inter-model differences -- specialty-triage supporting evidence.
