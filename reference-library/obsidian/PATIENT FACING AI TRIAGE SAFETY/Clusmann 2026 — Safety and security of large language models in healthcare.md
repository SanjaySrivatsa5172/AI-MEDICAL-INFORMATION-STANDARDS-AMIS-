# Safety and security of large language models in healthcare

**Clusmann J, Freyer O, Ostermann M, et al.** — *Nature* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Nature systematic review of the safety and security of clinical large language models -- a landmark-venue framing reference for this protocol's LLM-safety context. Maps security hazards to the development stages of clinical AI systems (design, data, model, inference, environment); identifies safety layers from core optimization objectives, knowledge integrity, and alignment through interaction with humans and systems; classifies threats by current clinical relevance; and surveys mitigation techniques with the corresponding stakeholder responsibilities. Broader than triage, but directly relevant to the study's safety rationale and to the failure surfaces (alignment, human-interaction, inference-time manipulation) that a patient-facing emergency-triage AI must withstand.
