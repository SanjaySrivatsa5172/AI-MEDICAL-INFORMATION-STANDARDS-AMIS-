# The role of large language models in emergency care: a comprehensive benchmarking study

**Naderi B, Liu L, Ghandehari A, et al.** — *npj Artificial Intelligence* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Two-part benchmarking study: (1) factual-knowledge testing of 18 LLMs on a curated MedMCQA subset spanning 12 ED chief complaints; (2) five models (GPT-5, GPT-4, Claude 3.5, Claude 4, LLaMA 3.1) evaluated on patient summaries, ESI scoring, investigative questioning, management planning, and differential diagnosis across 12 simulated ED cases. LLaMA-4 Maverick led factual accuracy (90.7%); GPT-5 led on the clinical-task battery.
