# Locally Deployable Small Language Models for Emergency Department Decision Support: A Systematic Benchmark of Fine-Tuning Strategies

**Zhang Q, Guo Y, Gong Y** — *arXiv preprint (accepted, AMIA 2026 Annual Symposium)* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Benchmarks 8 open-source small language models (zero-shot, prefix-tuning, LoRA, full fine-tuning) against Claude Haiku 4.5/Sonnet 4.5 baselines on 2,083 MIMIC-IV-ED cases for triage-level prediction, specialist-referral recommendation, and diagnosis prediction; LoRA-tuned SLMs outperformed the commercial baselines on triage-level prediction. Clinically-oriented benchmark methodology relevant to the trial's evaluation architecture.
