# The complexities of patient-centred conversational artificial intelligence

**Matos J, Buege O, Cheung D, Collins GS, Dhiman P, Li N, Mao B, Nelson BW, Ouroutzoglou M, Varghese P, Amar J** — *arXiv* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Analysis of 2,053 REAL patient-chatbot conversations showing wide variation in communication style and emotional expression; a patient SIMULATOR (separately modelling clinical content, emotional state, conversational strategy, and communication style; 15 human graders distinguished simulated from real at only 55%) applied across FIVE patient personae over 1,164 CLINICIAN-GRADED cases to evaluate four LLMs on URGENCY ASSESSMENT. Key finding: COMMUNICATION STYLE significantly alters triage outcomes -- systems evaluated only on idealised, articulate, cooperative simulated patients risk underperforming and AMPLIFYING DISPARITIES in the real world.
