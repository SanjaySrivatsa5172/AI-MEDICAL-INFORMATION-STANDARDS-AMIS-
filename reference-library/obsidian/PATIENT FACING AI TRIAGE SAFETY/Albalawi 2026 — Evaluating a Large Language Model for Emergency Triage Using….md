# Evaluating a Large Language Model for Emergency Triage Using Synthetic Clinical Dialogues: Toward a Multimodal Clinical Safety Framework

**Albalawi F, Alotaibi R, Alshammari S, Masri T** — *Diagnostics (Basel)* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Emergency-triage LLM clinical-SAFETY study: GPT-5.2 evaluated on 1000 balanced/stratified simulated Hajj/Umrah mass-gathering ED scenarios, combining synthetically generated clinical DIALOGUES (narrative input) with structured physiological indicators toward an ESI-based clinical-decision-support safety framework (ground truth derived from MIMIC-IV-ED). Incorporating clinical dialogues produced a 79.7% relative reduction in UNDER-TRIAGE across all ESI levels (91.9% in high-acuity cases) and a high-acuity recall of 94.5%, identifying critical 'red-flag' symptoms not captured in structured data. Directly on the protocol's over/under-triage failure mode and red-flag symptom recognition. Relevant via emergency-relevance (ESI emergency triage, ED, high-acuity, mass-gathering emergencies) + AI-safety FAILURE-MODE (undertriage reduction / red-flag identification / explicit 'clinical safety framework').
