# Performance and safety of a fine-tuned small language model for pediatric emergency triage: A benchmark study

**Lee EJ, Jung JY, Kim DK, Park JW, Kwak YH** — *PLOS One* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Real-world pediatric ED benchmark: a fine-tuned small language model did not surpass a structured-data XGBoost comparator overall but showed fewer extreme errors and lower strict under-triage in select high-acuity groups at the cost of higher over-triage -- proposed as a decision-support aid for human triage review rather than an autonomous triage system.
