# Performance of ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek for non-critical triage support using real-world conversations in emergency department

**Lee S, Jung S, Park JH, Cho H, Moon S, Ahn S.** — *BMC Emergency Medicine* (2025)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Evaluated GPT-4o/4.1/o3, Gemini 2.0/2.5, and DeepSeek V3/R1 on 1,057 real-world (unsummarized, conversational) ED triage dialogues from three South Korean tertiary hospitals rather than structured vignettes; Gemini 2.5 flash highest accuracy (73.8%); directly probes the lay/conversational-language dimension of triage safety
