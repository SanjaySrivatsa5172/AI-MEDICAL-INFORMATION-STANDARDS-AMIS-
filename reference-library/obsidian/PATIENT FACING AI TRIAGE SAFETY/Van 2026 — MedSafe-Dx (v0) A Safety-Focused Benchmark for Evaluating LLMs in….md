> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Preprint (medRxiv; Cortico Health Technologies): a deterministic, rules-based SAFETY benchmark scoring frontier LLMs on ESCALATION SENSITIVITY, avoidance of FALSE REASSURANCE, and uncertainty calibration over a fixed-seed 250-case sample of the January-2026 DDx Plus release; headline metric Triage Success Rate (TSR; net of unnecessary urgent calls against safe cases), secondary Safety Pass Rate (SPR). v3 (the version these figures come from) evaluates TWELVE models, GPT-5 Chat top on TSR at 72.4% (an earlier v2 counted 11 models with GPT-5.2 top SPR 97.6%); GPT-5.2's high SPR comes with ~71% over-escalation on non-urgent cases.
