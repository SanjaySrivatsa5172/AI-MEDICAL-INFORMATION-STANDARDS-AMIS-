# Evaluation format, not model capability, drives triage failure in the assessment of consumer health AI

**Fraile Navarro D, Magrabi F, Coiera E** — *arXiv (preprint)* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Partial replication of the Ramaswamy et al. Nature Medicine ChatGPT Health stress test: tested 5 frontier LLMs (GPT-5.2, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3 Flash, Gemini 3.1 Pro) on a 17-scenario bank under constrained exam-style (forced A/B/C/D, 1,275 trials) vs naturalistic patient-style-message (850 trials) conditions. Naturalistic interaction improved triage accuracy by 6.4 percentage points (p=0.015); forced-choice format was the dominant failure mechanism (three models scored 0-24% forced-choice vs 100% free-text on matched scenarios). Directly interrogates whether undertriage findings reflect model incapability or an artifact of exam-style evaluation format -- bears squarely on this protocol's dual-register, everyday-language design.
