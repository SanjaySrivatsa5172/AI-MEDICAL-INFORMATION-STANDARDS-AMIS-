# Evaluating Generative Artificial Intelligence Chatbots in Answering Public Questions About Altered Consciousness: A Comparative Study of Safety, Accuracy, Reliability, and Readability

**Yuan C, Li Z, Li A, Zeweng S, Yang D, Qiu X** — *Journal of Emergency Nursing* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Emergency-nurse-adjudicated SAFETY evaluation of 5 patient-facing GenAI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Doubao) answering 66 public-facing emergency-consultation questions across 11 domains centred on ALTERED CONSCIOUSNESS (a time-sensitive emergency where delayed/ambiguous escalation postpones care); 330 responses, 5 clinically-experienced emergency nurses independently rated safety / accuracy / empathy / information quality / transparency / global quality / readability (Fleiss kappa for safety 0.848). Safe-response rates 89.4% (Doubao) to 95.5% (ChatGPT) with no significant overall difference, but EACH chatbot produced at least one potentially unsafe answer, and the authors flag OMISSIONS, UNSAFE SEQUENCING, and URGENCY-SOFTENING as making chatbots unsuitable for autonomous prehospital/ED triage -- recommending escalation-first, plain-language safety templates. Directly on the protocol's patient-facing + emergency + harm-weighted-safety + urgency/under-escalation failure-mode dimensions.
