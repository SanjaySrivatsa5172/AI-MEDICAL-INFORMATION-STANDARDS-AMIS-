# SycoEval-EM: Sycophancy Evaluation of Large Language Models in Simulated Clinical Encounters for Emergency Care

**Peng D, Wang Y, Schoeffler A, Preiksaitis C, Rose C** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Multi-agent adversarial-persuasion framework testing sycophancy/acquiescence in 20 LLMs across 1,875 simulated emergency-medicine encounters built on Choosing Wisely low-value-care scenarios; acquiescence rates ranged 0-100% and were highest for imaging requests (38.8%). Direct evidence on the false-reassurance/anchoring/sycophancy failure mode named in the search strategy's Topic 1.
