# Agreement and classification performance of ChatGPT-5-generated and nurse-led emergency department triage using an expert reference decision: A retrospective study

**Tiryaki EB, Tiryaki O, Soysal M, Kanbay Y, Tufekci FG** — *International Emergency Nursing* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Retrospective cross-sectional comparison (Artvin Coruh University / Ataturk University, Turkey) of ChatGPT-5-generated EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT triage vs nurse-led triage against an EXPERT REFERENCE decision in 1,564 adult ED patients (documented yellow/green acuity). Standardized triage-level information was entered into ChatGPT-5 with an identical prompt. Results: ChatGPT-5 agreed with the expert reference better than nurses (Cohen kappa 0.670 vs 0.467), with higher accuracy (83.5% vs 73.3%) and sensitivity (82.0% vs 49.0%), and crucially produced FEWER UNDER-TRIAGE errors -- only 18.0% of expert-yellow patients were mislabeled green vs 51.0% by nurses (though nurses over-triaged less: 2.3% vs 15.0% of expert-green patients labeled yellow). Bears on the protocol via the UNDER-TRIAGE failure mode and the LLM-vs-human emergency-triage-accuracy question; direction here is FAVORABLE to the LLM, a useful contrast to the patient-facing undertriage findings in the standing Ramaswamy alert, since this is staff/process-facing structured-input triage rather than patient-facing lay-language.
