# Improving Trauma Triage Accuracy with Large Language Models: A Comparison to Human Expert Decisions

**Balaji AK, et al. (University at Buffalo Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences)** — *Journal of the American College of Surgeons* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Retrospective cohort of 133 pediatric trauma activations at a Level I center (Jan 2023-May 2025) comparing LLM-extracted 'Essential Transcripts' (Whisper-transcribed EMS call audio + named-entity recognition) against human-generated trauma-team pages and injury severity scores. LLM undertriage 4.8% vs human 5.1% (not statistically significant); LLM secondary-endpoint triage accuracy 83.5%, outperforming human clinicians. Prehospital/EMS-communication triage rather than patient-facing conversational triage, but bears directly on this protocol's core undertriage/overtriage axis and on LLM-vs-human-expert comparator methodology.
