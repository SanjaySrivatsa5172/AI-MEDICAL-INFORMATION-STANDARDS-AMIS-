# One-shot emergency psychiatric triage across 15 frontier AI chatbots

**Weilnhammer V, Luettgau L, Summerfield C, Sounderajah V, Wilkinson E, Corno V, Nour MM** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> 112 clinical vignettes tested as realistic single-message disclosures across 15 frontier AI chatbots, scored against a 4-level (routine / 1-week / 24-48h / emergency-now) urgency benchmark; emergency under-triage occurred in 5.6% (23/410) of level-D trials, all reassigned to the next-lower urgency tier, with overall accuracy ranging 42.0-71.8% across models. Closely parallels this protocol's own critical-miss-rate primary endpoint and single-message/everyday-language presentation design -- flagged as an urgent alert. Not yet peer-reviewed (arXiv preprint).
