> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Develops and applies a 10-item multidisciplinary evaluation rubric (accuracy, trustworthiness, empathy; binary met/not-met scoring) for grading a patient-facing chatbot's synthetic-scenario outputs before clinical deployment. Methodological example of clinician-authored rubric-based grading of patient-facing AI -- the Topic 2 evaluation-framework strand of this protocol.
