# Implicit Geographic Inference in LLM Medical Triage: Language-Driven Disparities in Emergency Recommendations

**Wong QH** — *arXiv* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Single-author arXiv preprint (submitted 2026-05-31): an identical neurological symptom profile (headache, blurred vision, nausea) was given to Gemini 3.5 Flash in six languages (n=450 calls); ER-visit recommendation rates ranged from 0% (Japanese, Hindi) to 30% (English, Arabic) despite near-identical severity scores, attributed to implicit GEOGRAPHIC inference from the input language, with a back-translation control supporting the mechanism. Bears on the input-register / language-sensitivity of triage disposition -- adjacent to the protocol's lay-vs-expert register question and its clinical-bias/fairness filter (undertriage driven by non-clinical input cues).
