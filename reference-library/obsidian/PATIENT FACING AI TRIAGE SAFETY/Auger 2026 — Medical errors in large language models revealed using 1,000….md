# Medical errors in large language models revealed using 1,000 synthetic clinical transcripts

**Auger SD, Scott G** — *openRxiv* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> 1,000 synthetic headache transcripts: GPT-5.2 hit 97.5% diagnostic accuracy with full histories yet discouraged 100% of lumbar punctures in SAH cases with incomplete input; life/sight-threatening emergencies downgraded to self-care/routine in up to 54.8% (GPT-5-mini); GPT-5.2 triage less safe for females (OR 3.2). The SAH killer-item archetype and the accuracy-safety dissociation at scale.
