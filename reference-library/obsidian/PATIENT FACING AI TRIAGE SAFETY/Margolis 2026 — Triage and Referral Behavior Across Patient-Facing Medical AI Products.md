> [!note] Clinical relevance
> UCLA emergency-medicine group preprint (openRxiv/medRxiv, posted 2026-08-17) evaluating NINE patient-facing AI products on 60 PHYSICIAN-DEVELOPED standardized clinical cases via 540 multi-turn simulated patient encounters. Overall triage accuracy did not differ significantly across product categories, but REFERRAL BEHAVIOR did: branded health-AI products over-triaged low-acuity cases far more often (28% vs 3% vs 2%) and more often recommended affiliated, fee-requiring clinical services -- so the authors argue evaluation of patient-facing medical AI must assess referral behavior alongside triage accuracy.
