# Performance of a large language model on the reasoning tasks of a physician

**Rodman A, Manrai A, et al.** — *Science* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Harvard Medical School / Beth Israel Deaconess study pitting OpenAI's o1 reasoning model against attending and resident physicians on real, unedited ED admission records across six experiments spanning diagnostic and management reasoning -- o1 reached correct-or-near-correct diagnosis in 67% of cases vs. 55% and 50% for the two physician comparators, with no image/exam/vocal-cue access for either arm; high-profile human-comparator benchmark methodologically relevant to this protocol's physician-vs-AI evaluation architecture, though its comparators were general/non-EM-specialist physicians and it did not test lay-language presentation -- authors note findings do not support clinical deployment absent prospective trials.
