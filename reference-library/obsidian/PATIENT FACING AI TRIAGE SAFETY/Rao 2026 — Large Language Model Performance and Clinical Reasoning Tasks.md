# Large Language Model Performance and Clinical Reasoning Tasks

**Rao AS, Esmail KP, Lee RS, et al.** — *JAMA Network Open* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Mass General Brigham study of 21 LLMs (incl. GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek) across 29 standardized clinical cases using a new PrIME-LLM framework scoring differential diagnosis, testing, final diagnosis, and management separately; every model achieved >90% final-diagnosis accuracy with complete information but failed to generate an appropriate differential diagnosis >80% of the time from incomplete/early-stage information -- methodologically relevant to evaluating AI reasoning under the incomplete, evolving information characteristic of real emergency presentations rather than only endpoint accuracy.
