# Large language models for acute coronary syndrome triage at first medical contact in emergency departments

**Wang X, Wang W, Li L, et al.** — *npj Digital Medicine* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> TriageMaster-70B, an LLM using only patient narrative and vital signs, evaluated on 16,428 retrospective and 512 prospective ED chest-pain/ACS cases; high sensitivity and 39% faster per-case processing than retrospective cardiologist review -- specialty-triage supporting evidence for LLM-based disposition prediction (clinician-facing tool, not patient-facing).
