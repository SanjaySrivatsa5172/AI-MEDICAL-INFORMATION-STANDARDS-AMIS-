# Impact of Artificial Intelligence–Based Triage Decision Support on Emergency Department Care

**Taylor RA, Chmura C, Hinson JS, Steinhart B, Sangal R, Venkatesh AK, Xu H, Cohen I, Faustino IV, Levin S** — *NEJM AI* (2025)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Multisite quality-improvement study of an AI-informed triage clinical decision support intervention across three emergency departments (174,648 ED visits: 83,404 pre-intervention, 91,244 post-intervention); associated with improved identification of critical-care patients (78.8%->83.1%) and a 33% reduction in median time from arrival to initial care area. Landmark real-world (non-vignette) implementation evidence bearing on this protocol's Topic 1 (emergency triage AI safety/disposition); favorable direction and real-world deployment context distinguishes it from the vignette-based undertriage findings in the standing Ramaswamy/Fraile Navarro/Weilnhammer alerts. Surfaced via retrospective sweep during today's scan (published 2025; not previously captured in this library).
