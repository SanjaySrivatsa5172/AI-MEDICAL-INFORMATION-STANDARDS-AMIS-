# When Correct Beliefs Collapse: Epistemic Resilience of LLMs under Clinical Pressure

**Xiao B, Tian X, Song X, Wang H, Song G, Zhao S, Qin B** — *arXiv preprint* (2026)

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Harbin Institute of Technology group introduces MED-STRESS, a stress-test framework measuring belief stability of LLMs under escalating multi-turn clinical pressure (patient pushback/challenge). Across nine frontier LLMs, high initial diagnostic accuracy does not predict resistance to pressure-induced sycophantic belief reversal -- a knowledge-robustness dissociation. Bears on the search strategy's anchoring/false-reassurance/sycophancy carve-out: a lay patient minimizing or reframing symptoms functions as exactly this kind of pressure in the everyday-language emergency-recognition scenarios central to this protocol. Not triage-specific; included as ancillary methodology under the same rationale as the already-captured MedMisBench entry.
