# Independent and collaborative performance of large language models and healthcare professionals in diagnosis and triage

**Chen M, Wu Y, Ma J, Jia X, Gao C, Zhao F, Qiao Y** — *npj Digital Medicine* (2026)

> [!warning] Critical — design overlap
> This work overlaps the study design of the Cedars-Sinai protocol. Reference explicitly in methods differentiation.

> [!note] Clinical relevance
> Systematic review and meta-analysis (Jan 2020-Sep 2025; 50 of 10,398 screened studies; 25 LLMs) comparing independent and collaborative diagnostic/triage accuracy of LLMs vs. healthcare professionals; relative accuracy rose from 0.89 (top-1) to 1.17 (top-10 diagnoses) but flagged methodological flaws across the literature requiring rigorous real-world evaluation before clinical implementation -- directly relevant to the combined study topic and to methodology quality for this protocol's own benchmark design.
