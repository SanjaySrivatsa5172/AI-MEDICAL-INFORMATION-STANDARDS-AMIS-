## What was found

1. **Jaarsma EH, Ring D, et al., *J Hand Surg Am* 2026** (PMID 42640232,
   doi 10.1016/j.jhsa.2026.07.014; peer-reviewed) — **the week's material finding**.
   GPT-5, 180 randomized vignettes, five benign upper-extremity conditions: 92% accuracy
   **unaffected by patient-stated self-diagnosis**; deviated from wrong self-diagnoses in
   65% of scenarios (89% of deviations correct). A genuine **contrasting null** to the
   Sheppert anchoring gap the Scientific Introduction cites. Falsifies nothing (different
   model generation; benign, non-disposition, no asymmetric harm) — but our own Ada-null
   precedent says competing nulls are declared, not omitted. → **EDIT 1 proposed.**

   sensitivity was **0.630**; lower-acuity discordance concentrated in older, diabetic,
   and trauma patients. Answering-side corroboration of the pending §7.3 amendment's
   tail-concentration argument (high aggregate agreement concealing subgroup-concentrated
   undertriage). No edit — supporting citation if the amendment is adopted.
