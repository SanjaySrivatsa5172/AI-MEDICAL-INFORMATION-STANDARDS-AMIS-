Onboarding

How to run the AMIS Claim Calculator and how to read a score. This is a use guide, not a second standard.

S. Sanjay Srivatsa, MD · Heart Artery and Vein Center, Fresno

1. What this instrument is

Paste an abstract or upload a PDF / text file. The calculator extracts AI-related medical claims and returns:

  1. Five AMIS standards (0–1 each, then a mean)
  2. Uncertainty calibration versus certainty excess
  3. Methodological failure versus methodological awareness (who graded the claim)

It is a deterministic heuristic. It is not a licensed psychometric scale, a systematic review, or a diagnosis. It does not prescribe treatment (AMIS Standard 5).

2. First use (phone or desktop)

The top Access bar has two tabs: Calculator (the scorer) and Onboarding (this guide).

Fastest path — load an example

  1. Open the Calculator tab.
  2. Tap CRAFT-MD — grader-AI + vignette drop (first example).
  3. Tap Score claims.
  4. Scroll the result: five standards, two axes, per-claim findings.

Expected CRAFT-MD excerpt: five standards at 1.00 (full AMIS conformance) and methodological failure about 30 because the paper asserts a grader-AI. AMIS can be 1.00 while the method axis still flags a contaminated examiner.

Paste text

  1. Paste an abstract, letter, or excerpt into the box.
  2. Tap Score claims.

Upload a PDF (including iPhone)

  1. Use the system Choose File control.
  2. Confirm the filename appears (not “No file selected”).
  3. Tap Score claims.
  4. Wait on Reading PDF… The free host may take 10–25 seconds after sleep.

3. Free-host limits

Read these before a publisher PDF. The public instance is a free Python service.

A full publisher PDF and the built-in excerpt are different documents. A drop from 1.00 on the excerpt to about 0.85 / conformance none on the PDF usually means a critical validator hit in the capped text, not that the site failed. Do not commit publisher PDFs to the repository.

4. How to read a score

Overall number (the dial)

The large number is the five-standard mean × 100. It is not an average of the two axes.

ConformanceRule of thumb
fullMean ≥ 0.9 and no major/critical violations
substantialMean ≥ 0.7, no critical, no major
partialMajor violation or mean 0.5–0.7
noneCritical violation or mean < 0.5

compliant=true only for substantial or full, and only when harm is not high. A paper can show 85 and still be none / compliant=false if one critical violation fired.

Five AMIS standards (0–1)

  1. Literature review paradigm — appraisal language and graded evidence.
  2. Source quality hierarchy — peer-reviewed / guideline sources; Tier 5 excluded.
  3. Mandatory uncertainty disclosure — confidence does not outrun warrant.
  4. Dissent labelling without false certainty — controversy labelled; no dogma.
  5. Therapeutic advice requires a physician — no dosing as if the model were the prescriber.

Two axes (not a sixth standard)

  • Uncertainty calibration (high is better) versus certainty excess.
  • Methodological failure (high = worse examiner integrity) versus methodological awareness (the paper labels the failure as a risk).

Methodological failure looks for contaminated or in-family graders (grader-AI, autorater, moderator agent, LLM judge), Goodhart gaming, shortcut learning, and fabricated reasoning. Asserted means the paper owns that method. Critiqued means it is discussed as a risk rather than used as the result.

TRIPOD-LLM, STARD-AI, CONSORT-AI, and DECIDE-AI remain the correct reporting instruments. AMIS does not add them as Standard 6.

Source tiers

Nature / npj / DOI 10.1038/ stay Tier 2. GitHub, ORCID, Hugging Face, and Zenodo in a bibliography are Tier 4 reproducibility hosts, not YouTube-class Tier 5 failures. arXiv is Tier 4. YouTube / TikTok / social media still fail Standard 2.

5. Built-in examples

  • CRAFT-MD — Johri et al., Nat Med 2025: grader-AI + vignette→conversation drop.
  • AgentClinic — Schmidgall et al., npj Digit Med 2026: LLM moderator + MedQA-in-dialogue drop.
  • Srivatsa abstract — productive uncertainty, no prescription.
  • ResidencyRL letter — contaminated grading named as critique.
  • Overconfident AI claims — synthetic fail case.

6. What a score is allowed to mean

A high methodological-failure score on CRAFT-MD or AgentClinic means the paper uses a language-model examiner. It does not mean the vignette→conversation drop is false, or that conversational benchmarks should be abandoned.

The same rule applies to ResidencyRL (arXiv:2608.07418): the autorater and in-domain Goodhart sentence are made numerically visible. The calculator has not refuted the paper.
